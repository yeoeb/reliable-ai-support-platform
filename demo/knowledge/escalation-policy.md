# Support escalation policy

Escalate an incident to the security queue immediately when it involves a
suspected account takeover, exposed credentials, malware, or unauthorized
access to protected data.

Escalate a service outage to the operations queue when multiple users are
affected or a critical workflow is unavailable. Include the observed symptoms,
start time, affected service, and troubleshooting already completed.

Do not copy passwords, access tokens, private keys, or unnecessary personal data
into an escalation. High-risk or mutating actions still require their normal
authorization and approval checks.
