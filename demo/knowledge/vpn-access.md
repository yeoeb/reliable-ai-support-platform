# VPN access

Confirm that the employee has an active account and an enrolled multifactor
authentication method before troubleshooting VPN access.

For connection failures, verify network connectivity, confirm the approved VPN
client is current, and retry after signing out of the client. Do not disable
multifactor authentication or certificate validation as a workaround.

Escalate persistent certificate errors, access for a new protected resource, or
a suspected compromised device to the security queue.
